In one sentence

A booking desk that is open all day, in the client's own chat

The assistant takes a booking request, collects the papers, tracks the shipment, and hands anything else to a person. Every decision that matters comes from MKY's own records, not from guesswork.

Where

Telegram, and the chat window on the website. The same conversation, the same rules, on both.

Language

Every message is in Egyptian Arabic and English at once, so a card can be forwarded to a driver, a broker or a customs agent.

When

Bookings and tracking, any hour. A person from 9 AM to 7 PM Cairo time — and a direct number for anything that cannot wait.

Step 1 of 3

Your details

  • Name — the client name the booking is registered under.
  • Mobile number — shared with Telegram's "Share my number" button (verified), typed in any digit system, or offered back from the last booking: "Is +20… still your number? Reply yes."
  • Both can come in one message: "Ariful and my number is +49 176 …" is read as a name and a number.
  • The number is kept on the client's record. When they later ask for an agent, they are not asked for it again.

Step 2 of 3

The vehicle

The chassis number is checked first

New nothing on file — continue.

Known, not booked we hold the unit — the make is filled in from our records.

Already booked a live booking exists — the client is given its reference and route, and no second request is created. This check runs wherever the chassis appears, even inside a pasted block.

Then the rest

  • Make (and model) — "مرسيدس أكتروس" becomes Mercedes-Benz Actros on the paperwork.
  • Port of loading — any city or port in Europe.
  • Egyptian destination — one of the five we serve: Alexandria (incl. El Dekheila), Port Said, Damietta, Ain Sokhna, Suez. Anything else is refused with the list.
  • MRN — "I already have one" or "I need MKY to issue it", which opens a request for the customs desk.

Step 2 of 3

The papers

What is asked for

  • Commercial invoice
  • Transport document (CMR / brief)
  • MRN export declaration — unless MKY is obtaining the MRN

What happens to each file

  • It is read automatically and its type recognised — a scan or a phone photo works too.
  • The chassis on the paper is checked against the booking. A paper for another vehicle is flagged, not counted.
  • A file the assistant cannot make out is asked about by name: "I have scan-1.pdf, but I am not sure what it is."
  • The assistant says received. Only your team says verified.

Everything at once

The client can attach the three files to one message and write the details on it. Several files sent together get one answer, after all of them are read.

[ 3 files ] Rotterdam to Damietta. DAF XF 480 FT, client Giza Freight Lines, chassis XLRTEH4350G741552
✅ Received: Brief, Invoice, MRN. 📝 Noted from your message: client Giza Freight Lines · chassis XLRTEH4350G741552 · make DAF XF 480 FT · route Rotterdam → Damietta Port. ✅ Perfect! We have everything we need. Step 3 of 3 — your booking.

Step 3 of 3

Your booking

  • A summary card built from exactly what the client gave: client, phone, chassis, make, route, and each document with a tick.
  • Edit any line — a changed chassis is checked again, a changed route re-asks both ports.
  • Confirm — the request is sent to Operations in one step that cannot be done twice, however many times the button is pressed.
  • The client immediately gets the booking reference in the reply and a PDF copy of the request, headed "Awaiting confirmation".
  • A status card is pinned to the top of the chat and follows the vehicle until delivery.

Your side

What happens at the desk after Confirm

Review

The request appears in the Booking Queue with its documents. Each paper can be verified, or rejected with a reason — the client is told and sends a replacement in the same chat.

→

Ask the client

"Request info" parks the request as waiting for client and messages them. Their reply — text or a file — brings it back to the queue with a "client responded" badge.

→

Confirm or reject

Confirm opens the shipment and sends the client a confirmation and a second PDF, headed "Confirmed". Reject sends the reason and a way to reach the team.

Every message to the client is queued and retried until it is delivered, and never sent twice. A booking is never lost because an email or a Telegram call failed.

Tracking

Track my shipment

  • The client sends a chassis number or a booking reference — in a sentence, with a label, in Arabic, in any spacing.
  • The card shows status, vessel, current location, route, expected arrival and the last update — every value from MKY's records, or "Not available yet". Nothing is guessed.
  • Refresh re-reads the records rather than repeating the last answer.
  • A booking that has been confirmed but not yet shipped says so, instead of showing empty fields.
  • A client sees only their own shipments. A reference that belongs to somebody else gets the same "not found" as a wrong one.

Trust

Rules the assistant never breaks

1

It never invents. No phone number, price, date or customs requirement is made up. If something is not configured, it says so.

2

One chassis, one booking. A unit with a live booking cannot be booked again — the rule is enforced three times over, down to the database itself.

3

Received is not verified. The assistant confirms a file arrived. Only your team declares it correct.

4

Your own shipments only. A forwarded reference cannot be used to read another company's route or schedule.

5

Decisions come from records. The AI reads a chassis out of a sentence and answers questions. Whether a booking may be made, what is missing, and what goes to Operations is decided from MKY's data — and tested, 237 times, without any AI in the loop.

6

Every action is logged. Who did what, when — without document contents or secrets.

For discussion

Does this match how MKY works?

1. Are the three steps in the right order — the person first, then the vehicle, then the booking?

2. Documents: invoice, transport document and MRN. Should ACID or EUR.1 be required at booking time?

3. When MKY obtains the MRN, what exactly is needed from the client? The assistant currently asks for a free description and raises a task.

4. Desk hours: 9 AM to 7 PM, every day? Which days off? Whose direct number goes to urgent after-hours clients?

5. General questions ("how long to Alexandria?") are answered by the AI from MKY's documents. Should they go to an agent instead?

6. The five Egyptian ports — complete? Any European loading points to refuse?

7. How long may a new request wait before it counts as overdue on the dashboard?

8. Who is told about a new booking: which email inbox, which staff Telegram group?
